CHAPTER 2 — WALK THE MANOR AS GARY (Phase 1 · playable spike)

what is this?

You are Gary the ghost. Drift room to room — the manor you once explored in text now develops in 8-bit as you enter each room, while places you haven't visited stay fogged. Arrow keys / WASD or click a lit room to move; click a room you've seen to inspect it (description + items appear on the right); take the green stairs buttons to change floors; press B to drop bait and lure the guest. Turn on reveal all for a pure map view of how the whole manor connects. Fully isolated under web/spike/ — the shipped game and its tests are untouched.
