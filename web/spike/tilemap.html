CHAPTER 2 — WALK THE MANOR AS GARY (Phase 1 · playable spike)

You are Gary the ghost. Drift room to room — the manor you once explored in text now develops in 8-bit as you enter each room, while places you haven't visited stay fogged. Arrow keys / WASD or click a lit room to move; take the green stairs buttons to change floors. Still fully isolated under web/spike/ — the shipped game and its tests are untouched.

The fun kernel we're testing: re-experiencing familiar rooms in a new form. You saw the Royal Hall, the Cellar, Dreadmaw's cave as ASCII in Chapter 1; here they redraw in 8-bit the moment you step in — a fresh view of ground you already know.

Next layer (already wired in tilemap.js, not turned on yet): a wandering Guest you manipulate — drop bait and they path toward it (guestStep). We'll switch that on once moving around already feels good on its own.

Art: click load atlas… to swap a different Firefly/NanoBanana tileset live (the bundled one loads by default).
