CHAPTER 2 — WALK THE MANOR AS GARY (2D map)

what is this?

You are Gary the ghost. Drift room to room — the manor you once explored in text now develops in 8-bit as you enter each room, while places you haven't visited stay fogged. Move with arrow keys / WASD, click any connected room to walk there, or type commands in the bar below ("go library", "n", "drop bait", "look", "help"). Hover a room you've seen to read its description + items on the right. Press B to drop bait and lure the guest. Switch labels between vector (crisp) and 8-bit, or turn on reveal all for a pure map view of how the whole manor connects. Shipped in the app under web/view2d/ and reachable from the text game via the 2D link.

    BLACKWOOD MANOR

    Thirteen heirlooms. One cursed clock. One restless ghost.

    You are Gary — dead, but not gone. Bound to the manor you once explored in life, you drift its fog-drowned halls where thirteen lost Blackwood heirlooms still wait in the dark. Find them. Carry them home. Make the house whole again… before it makes you part of it.

    Your quest: enshrine all thirteen relics in the Reliquary's recesses. Set the last one in place and they fuse into the Countdown Clock — and the manor tolls its thirteenth hour.

    Two ways to play

    📜 Text mode switch →

    Old-school adventure. Type what Gary does — "go library", "take locket", "drop bait" — or tap the arrows. Read the room. Trust your gut. Click to play in text.

    🗺️ 2D map mode play →

    The manor paints itself in 8-bit as you step into each room; everywhere you haven't been stays lost in fog. Click a room to walk there, use arrow keys / WASD, or hit ▶ auto-tour. Click to close and explore.

    The thirteen heirlooms

    Hunt down every one. They ride in your Carrying satchel, then slide into the Reliquary the moment they're enshrined. Hover (or tap) any relic for its story.
