CHAPTER 2 — WALK THE MANOR AS GARY (Phase 1 · playable spike)

what is this?

You are Gary the ghost. Drift room to room — the manor you once explored in text now develops in 8-bit as you enter each room, while places you haven't visited stay fogged. Move with arrow keys / WASD, click any connected room to walk there, or type commands in the bar below ("go library", "n", "drop bait", "look", "help"). Hover a room you've seen to read its description + items on the right. Press B to drop bait and lure the guest. Switch labels between vector (crisp) and 8-bit, or turn on reveal all for a pure map view of how the whole manor connects. Shipped in the app under web/view2d/ and reachable from the text game via the 2D link.
