# The Upside Down and Doomsday Clock canon

This is the player-facing text and constant contract for Lanes 2 through 10. The [master plan](2026-11-07_plan_todo_DoomsdayClock.md) owns scope and scores; this document fixes names, grammar, prose, puzzle outcomes, and the final values of its decisions.

## Shared constraints

* Keep puzzle handlers and prose in the content modules. Generic verbs alone belong in the parser and commands layer. Each new heirloom has `pendingHeirloom: true` until Lane 10 enables all eleven as treasures together.
* Every punitive outcome must be foreshadowed before it occurs. Hidden sight from the high, XRAY GOGGLES, or OBSIDIAN EYE automatically reveals what SEARCH would find, including traversable revealed exits.
* The realm is THE UPSIDE DOWN. Its six heirlooms and the manor's five join the existing thirteen to make the twenty-four-hour DOOMSDAY CLOCK. Flight works in the realm without special rules.
* Tone: fair classic adventure puzzles with absurd schoolboy comedy and affectionate Stranger Things and Narnia nicknames. No holiday theme, recycled food gag as a mechanic, or reproduced song lyrics.

## Realm rooms and passage

* The MINERS' LOCKER in the MINING GALLERY opens into WAYLAMP CLEARING after DREADMAW moves. OUT from the clearing returns to the gallery. The realm connects on foot to the lift's BELOW stop, which holds the LOWER PANEL.

## Realm puzzles

* Feed DEMOGORGONZOLA LIMBURGER, ÉPOISSES, and MUNSTER in any order for the DEMOGORGONZOLA RIND; other interactions swallow and eject the player to the FRONT GATE with inventory intact.
* Solve three consecutive CABINET RADIO rounds for the PORTABLE TRANSISTOR RADIO. Each round offers five numbered real 1980s song titles and one original literal clue.
* The PLANETARIUM's fixed five-number projector drops the GOLD EYEPIECE on the first safe selection; the MAP ROOM holds the STELLAR MAP. Number 2 ejects the player; number 4 ejects the player still burning.
* Spell FART, STINK, or CHEESE on the SHACK wall for the PURPLE CRAYON. The KID'S DRAWING hangs on its door.
* Extinguish the UPSIDE-DOWN CAMPFIRE to uncover the CYAN GEM. The LOWER PANEL starts MAGENTA top, empty middle, YELLOW bottom; its solution is YELLOW top, MAGENTA middle, CYAN bottom.

## Manor puzzles and shared high

* The BLACKWOOD DIAMOND comes from a single lump of PARLOR COAL in the KITCHEN MICROWAVE set to B. The SILVER WEATHERCOCK, SNUFFBOX, BARONESS PHOTOGRAPH, and CORKSCREW complete the quintet.
* Fresh and dried mushrooms, snuff, and wine start one shared high with source-specific openings and a twelve-line cycling reaction pool; HUD indication remains 🍄.

## Scores and decisions

* All awards come exclusively from master Section 8, once per persisted award flag. Master Section 13 decisions D1 through D37 are resolved; their exact final values are recorded below as the contract is completed.
